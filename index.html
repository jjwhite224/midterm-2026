02 — THE METHOD

Transparent
by design.

Polling data are samples, not election results. A transparent explorer should expose its assumptions and its limits instead of suggesting false precision.

01

Check the underlying source

Every real poll links back to its release. The data pipeline checks dates, values, duplicates, and response choices.

02

Choose a descriptive average

Compare equal weights to recency weighting. Neither approach corrects for house effects, selection bias, or correlated error.

03

Preserve a dated snapshot

To avoid hindsight, any authentic pre-election archive must be collected and committed before voting. No pre-election snapshot has been generated in this starter.

04

Audit afterward

Future releases can compare frozen inputs and models against results, explicitly distinguishing provisional counts from certified returns.
