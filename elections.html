What the tally means

Each race is assigned to the party with more than a 50% model chance. Exact ties stay unresolved. Senate totals include the 65 seats not up for election. This deterministic tally is not the publisher’s simulated median, expected seat count, or probability of chamber control.

The two current independent senators caucus with Democrats. Independent challengers remain independent in the tally; their future caucus is not assumed.

Where the numbers come from

Polling Forecast’s October 31 export supplies the race forecasts under CC BY 4.0. We regrouped these outputs by state and counted race favorites. The publisher’s method blends polling with national trends, political lean and incumbency. No-poll races are model estimates, not observed voter preferences.

Membership comes from the House and Senate. House membership and the model’s incumbent labels differ, so we compare chamber totals, not claimed district flips. All 435 voting House seats are up; nonvoting delegates are excluded.
